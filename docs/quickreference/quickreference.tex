Sys.g\_tri & diagonal elements of the g-tensor of a triplet & list of floats\\

\midrule

Sys.donor\_list & index of cores assigned to the donor & list of str/int\\
Sys.acceptor\_list & index of cores assigned to the acceptor & list of str/int\\

Sys.A$i$\_frame & euler angles, rotate A$_i$-tensor to the lab. frame & list of floats\\
Sys.I$i$ & spin quantum number of core group $i$ (integer or half) & float  \\
Sys.n$i$ & number of cores in core group $i$ & integer \\
Sys.A & diagonal elements of all A-tensors (assigned to spins $i$ and optionally $j$) & list [[$A_i$], [$A_j$]]\\
Sys.A\_frame & euler angles, rotate all A-tensors to the lab. frame & list [[$Fr_i$], [$Fr_j$]]\\
Sys.I & spin quantum numbers of all cores & list [[$A_i$], [$A_j$]] \\

\midrule

Sys.J\_ex & exchange-coupling constant $J$ of two interacting electronic species $J$ in MHz & float\\
Sys.D\_tri & zero-field-splitting constant $D$ of a triplet in MHz & float\\
Sys.E\_tri & zero-field-splitting constant $E$ of a triplet in MHz & float\\
Sys.D\_frame & euler angles, rotate D-tensor to lab. frame & list of floats\\
Sys.D\_tri\_frame & euler angles, rotate triplet D-tensor to lab. frame & list of floats\\

SimOpt.theta & User-chosen orientation-angles for theta if SimOpt.grid = "single" & list \\
SimOpt.phi & User-chosen orientation-angles for phi if SimOpt.grid = "single" & list \\

SimOpt.pop\_evolution & Decides wether the population evolution is calculated and returned or not & boolean\\
SimOpt.eigval\_mode & Decides wether only the eigenvalues shall be calculated & boolean\\
SimOpt.cpu\_cores & Number of CPU cores used for time evolution, 0 uses all available cores & integer \\
SimOpt.CUPY & Use GPU and Cupy for faster calcuations & boolean\\
